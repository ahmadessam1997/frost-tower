This policy covers both versions of Frost Tower: the Android version on Google Play and the iPhone version on the App Store. They are the same game; where a platform service differs, this page says which platform each statement applies to.

The short version

Frost Tower has no accounts and no login. Your climbs, scores, snowflakes and unlocked skins are stored on your device only.

The game does four things that involve other companies: it shows ads (Google AdMob on both platforms), it sells optional one-time purchases (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone, with entitlements managed through RevenueCat on both), it measures how the game is played (Google Analytics for Firebase), and it offers an optional global ranking (Google Play Games on Android, Apple Game Center on iPhone). Those services receive some data about your device. Everything they receive is described below.

The game never asks for and never receives your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera. Payment card details are entered into Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone), never into Frost Tower — we never see them.

Ads on Android and iPhone

Both versions show the same ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown first and you can change your answer later from Privacy choices in the shop.

Android: the game declares the AD_ID permission so that AdMob can read the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete that ID in Android settings (see Your choices below).

iPhone: there is no AD_ID permission. After the consent form, the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised and the IDFA is never read. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use the IDFA for personalised ads. Google's SKAdNetwork is used for ad attribution on iPhone; it reports to the advertiser that an install came from an ad without identifying you.

Reward videos are optional

Frost Tower shows a banner on its menu screens and a full-screen ad between some climbs. It also offers optional reward videos that you choose to watch in exchange for snowflakes, one revive, or doubling a run's snowflakes. You are never forced to watch a reward video, and declining one costs you nothing but the reward.

Removing ads

The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes every ad permanently on any device signed in to the same store account (your Google Play account on Android, your Apple Account on iPhone): the banner, the ads between climbs, and the optional reward videos too. Once ads are removed, the ads SDK is no longer asked to load ads. On iPhone the payment is processed by Apple and the purchase is tied to your Apple Account; a Restore purchases button in the shop brings it back after a reinstall. On both platforms RevenueCat keeps track of which unlocks you own.

The rating prompt

After a few climbs the game may ask whether you would like to rate it. On Android this is a dialog drawn by the game that only opens the Google Play listing if you accept. On iPhone the game asks Apple to show its own review request sheet instead; that sheet is handled by Apple and collects nothing for us. In both cases the only thing recorded is the analytics event that the prompt was answered (see the table above).

Notifications

Frost Tower can show one reminder notification about your daily mission, about a day after you last played. It is created and scheduled entirely on your device — no push service is used and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else to deliver it. This is the same on Android and iPhone. On Android 13 and later, and on iPhone, the game asks for the notification permission only when you claim your first daily-mission reward, never at launch. You can refuse, and you can turn the reminder off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Frost Tower → Notifications or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Frost Tower; the game works the same without it.

Children

Frost Tower is not directed at children under 13. The ad SDK is configured to request only G-rated ad content. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want any associated advertising identifier removed, contact us at the address above.

Your choices and how to delete data

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads (Android): Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can delete the advertising ID entirely there; ads will still appear but will not be personalised.
  • Withdraw tracking permission (iPhone): iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, switch off Frost Tower (or disable “Allow Apps to Request to Track” for every app). Ads will still appear but will not be personalised and the IDFA is no longer read.
  • Ad consent (both platforms): where Google's consent form was shown, you can change your answer from Privacy choices in the shop.
  • Delete everything stored on the device: uninstalling Frost Tower erases your scores, snowflakes, power-ups, mission streak and unlocked skins permanently. There is no cloud copy for us to keep.
  • Gameplay analytics: these are tied to the advertising ID and an app-instance ID, not to your name. Deleting the advertising ID or withdrawing tracking permission (above) and uninstalling the app cuts the link; to ask for deletion of analytics data, use our data deletion page.
  • Global ranking (Android): your entry can be removed from within the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data).
  • Global ranking (iPhone): your Game Center entry is held by Apple under Apple's Game Center privacy terms. You can sign out of Game Center or manage your Game Center profile in iOS Settings → Game Center, and request your Game Center data from Apple at privacy.apple.com. We never receive or store your Game Center player ID.
  • Purchase records: these are held by the store you bought from (Google Play on Android, Apple on iPhone) and by RevenueCat as proof of purchase. Request deletion at our data deletion page. Note that deleting a purchase record means the associated unlock can no longer be restored.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iPhone) under their own policies.

Data retention

We operate no servers and keep no database of our own. Retention is governed by the processors: Google's advertising data retention policy for ad data, Google Analytics for Firebase's retention setting for gameplay events (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting), RevenueCat's for purchase records, and Apple's for App Store purchase records and Game Center scores on iPhone. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data immediately.
